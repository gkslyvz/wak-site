The Ancestors' Shelf

The old books we quote,
and the quiet things in our films.

Everything we post leans on a handful of very old books and a few very simple objects. People keep asking where to find them, so here they are, with a line on why each one earns its place.

Honest note. Some links on this page are affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We only list things we would put on our own shelf, and nothing here promises luck, wealth or healing. An hourglass is just an hourglass. What you do with the hour is the point.

The books we quote

Every quote in our posts comes from public-domain translations of these works. A paper copy is still the best way to read them slowly.

Marcus Aurelius · Stoic

Meditations

A Roman emperor's private notes to himself about anger, fear, death and doing the next right thing. Short entries, good for one page a night.

Find a copy →
Lao Tzu · Taoist

Tao Te Ching

Eighty-one short chapters on softness, enough and letting go. We quote James Legge's 1891 translation.

Find a copy →
Rumi · Sufi

The Masnavi

The reed flute's lament for the reed bed, and hundreds of stories after it. A book to keep beside the bed, not to finish.

Find a copy →
Augustine · Christian

Confessions

“Our heart is restless, until it repose in Thee.” The first real autobiography, written by a man who wasted years and said so.

Find a copy →
Thomas à Kempis · Christian

The Imitation of Christ

Five-hundred-year-old advice against vanity and the hunger to be praised. Surprisingly sharp for the age of the feed.

Find a copy →
Saadi · Persian

The Gulistan (Rose Garden)

Short tales and couplets on kings, beggars, friendship and the tongue. Each one fits in a minute.

Find a copy →
