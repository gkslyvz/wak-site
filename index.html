Our promise

Every quote is cited. Every story is retold in our own words from public-domain sources. We do not promise miracles, cures or fortunes. We offer what people have always passed down: a way to get through tonight, and a reason to try again tomorrow.

What the Ancestors Knew shares stories for reflection. It is not religious instruction and is not a substitute for medical, psychological or professional advice.
